# NNNN. The decision in one line

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md) | rejected
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who decided
- **Supersedes / amends:** a record id, or "nothing"

## Context and problem statement

What forced a decision, in two or three sentences: the forces, the constraint, and what goes wrong
without one. Link the code, incident record or measurement it came from.

## Considered options

Each option carries **what it buys and what it costs**. Keep the options that lost - "we tried that" is
worth an answer with a reason. An option that a measurement settled says which measurement.

## Decision

Present tense. Why it beat the others. One decision per record.

## Consequences

Both the good and the bad - a record is not marketing.

**What enforces it:** the test, hook, schema or written rule that holds this. A decision nothing
enforces is a wish.
